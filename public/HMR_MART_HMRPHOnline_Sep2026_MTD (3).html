Key Definitions

"Real Orders Received" (Fulfillment tab, September KPI) 88 raw orders (deduped) → minus 5 dev/test-tagged cancellations → minus 3 confirmed customer-initiated cancellations → minus 0 genuine duplicate retries → 80. System-initiated (expired) cancellations and the 1 no-reason-logged cancellation stay inside this count, same as any other real demand that entered the funnel.
"Fulfilled" An order counts as fulfilled if its order_no is found directly in xv3.mart_net_sales (sales_channel = HMRPH ONLINE, transaction_type = sale), or if a "probable match" is found: same customer name (accent/case/punctuation normalized) + invoice dated within roughly -1 to +10 days of the order + invoice amount at or below the order amount by no more than ₱500 (a plausible delivery-fee gap — see the data quality note below). The order_status text field is not used for this — it does not reliably update once an order is actually sold.
Pick rate — intentionally excluded HMR MART fulfills through its own WMS, not the PickApp flow used by BOPIS branches, so picking_status on these orders is not meaningful and is left out of every metric in this report.
Cancellation Reasons — 7 categories Built from the free-text cancellation_reason field (~30 distinct values customers can pick from): System-Initiated (Expired) — reason contains "expired"; Payment Issues — payment method/processing/COD-mismatch reasons; Technical / Website Issues — checkout, login, cart, or shipping-load failures; Changed Mind / No Longer Needed — genuine customer change of heart; Order Modification — duplicate orders, adding items, promo-code fixes; No Reason Logged — null; Other / Miscellaneous — everything else.
Return Reasons — 10 categories Built from invoice_remarks (free text, inconsistent RET/REF prefixes and spelling): Cancelled by Customer, Refused/Could Not Deliver (Consignee), Not Working/Defective, Damaged Items, No Actual Items (Shortage), Other/Miscellaneous, Wrong Item/Order Error, Wrong Size, No Reason Logged, Address Issue.

Known Data Quality Issues

order_status does not reliably reflect fulfillment Many orders sit at "Paid" or "Processing" in order_status long after they've actually been invoiced and sold. Always determine fulfillment via mart_net_sales, never via this field.
Some invoices have no order_no populated 194 invoices across June–September had a blank order_no despite belonging to a real order. Root cause (confirmed by a dev spot-check): the invoiced amount often runs ₱80–₱375 below the order's net_total — most likely a delivery/shipping fee included in the order total but not captured in the invoice line value. Reconciled 172 of the 194 (88.7%) by matching on customer name + date + this fee-adjusted amount gap; 22 remain genuinely unmatched.
Name-based matching gets unreliable for heavy repeat customers A handful of customers (e.g. KESHIAH BONIFACIO, NATHALI ONG, RICHARD VERDEBLANCO) placed many orders and generated many invoices within the same short window. For these, name + date + amount can no longer uniquely identify the right invoice — at least one exact-amount match was found dated 23 days after its order, well outside the ±10-day matching window used elsewhere. These cases need manual reconciliation rather than the automated pass.
Two orders point at what may be an order_no off-by-one Orders 248158 and 248253 each have exactly one invoice under that customer's name, but that invoice is linked to a different, adjacent order_no (248156 and 248252 respectively — 1-2 numbers off). Possibly a linking bug at invoicing time; flagged for dev rather than assumed either way.
Dev/test data mixed into live tables Customer_id 70700 tags its own cancellations "Dev test"/"Devtest"/"devtest" and has at least one untagged order (250960, ₱3.00) still sitting live. Customer name "TEST ACCOUNT" appears in August's cancellations (2 orders) and again in an unresolved "Paid, no invoice" order. Also excluded: cancellation reason "FOR TESTING" (June, 1 order). All excluded from "real" counts throughout this report.
Live data drifts between queries This is a live warehouse, and this report was built over a long session. September's cancelled-order count grew from 19 to 21 partway through — one new cancellation landed mid-session. The Fulfillment tab's KPI cards are held at the original 19 for internal consistency with the rest of that tab's math (Real Orders Received = 80, etc.); the Cancellation tab is built fresh and states its own count.
